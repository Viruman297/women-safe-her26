🛡️ Safety Tips

Stay Alert • Stay Safe

🛡️
Stay Aware
Avoid distractions while walking alone. Stay alert to your surroundings and trust your instincts.
🚕
Verify Transport
Check vehicle number plates and driver details before entering any cab or ride service.
📱
Share Location
Share your live location with trusted contacts whenever traveling alone.
🚨
Call Help Early
If you feel unsafe, contact emergency services immediately. Don't wait for the situation to worsen.

🚑 Emergency Advice

  • Stay calm and move to a safe place.
  • Use the SOS button immediately.
  • Call trusted contacts.
  • Share your live location.
  • Contact Police (112) if needed.
